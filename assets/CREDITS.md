## Art

World backgrounds (`assets/backgrounds/*.jpg`, originals in `concept/`) and the app icon were painted for this game
with ChatGPT image generation on the owner's account. Bottles, corks and shelves are built procedurally in Blender
(`blender/scripts/build_assets.py`); liquids, glass, streams and particles are shaders and code.
